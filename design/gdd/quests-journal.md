# Loops That Help Blood Sugar

Code: `src/data/insights.js`. Each loop is built on a self-management habit with evidence behind it:
looking at patterns by time of day, correcting quickly, reflecting on what helped, and noticing
stable stretches. All of them are goals or observations, never instructions (safety.md #10).

## 1. Maple's weekly goal (pattern quest)

- At the start of each week (Monday, the player's time zone), look at the 14 days before it, split
  into overnight (12–6am), morning, afternoon and evening.
- If any window has more than 4% below range, the goal is **N nights/mornings… without lows**.
- Otherwise the weakest window by time in range becomes **N steady mornings**, with a target a step
  above the recent average (+5, rounded to 5, between 60% and 85%), so it's reachable.
- N = 3 of 7. Each **Sick** journal tag that week lowers N by one (minimum 1).
- Reward: 75 sunlight and a ribbon. Maple explains why ("Your mornings were 58% in range over the
  last two weeks").

## 2. Comebacks

Back in range within 60 minutes of leaving it (high or low) is a comeback: a sparkle, a toast and the
recovery bonus already in the Sun Well (capped at 4 a day; see sunlight.md). Lows never earn; only
the return does.

## 3. Steady streak

3+ hours in range without a break brings out butterflies. The reading chip shows "2h 40m steady"
after the first hour.

## 4. Morning recap

The first open each day shows yesterday's plant growing in, with % in range, comebacks and the best
time of day, then offers to tag the day.

## 5. Garden Journal

- Tags: Walked, Exercise, Pre-bolused, Lighter meals, Slept well, Stressed, Sick, New site or sensor.
- Tag today or any of the last 6 days. First tags on a day give 10 sunlight once (a thank-you for
  reflecting).
- **Insights:** for each tag with 3+ tagged days and 3+ other journaled days, compare average time in
  range ("On days tagged Walked, you averaged 78% in range vs 64%"). Only shown when the gap is 5
  points or more. Compared only against days the player journaled, so it's apples to apples.
- Owner only. Never shared with visitors.
